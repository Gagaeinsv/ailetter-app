# AI-Letter: Product Overview & Brand Positioning

## Product Vision
**AI-Letter** is a unified career suite designed to empower job hunters and freelancers. Instead of dealing with disparate tools for resume building, cover letter drafting, application tracking, and interview preparation, AI-Letter consolidates the entire workflow into a single, cohesive, AI-augmented dashboard.

---

## Core Value Propositions

### 1. High-End Visual Design & Typography
* Unlike standard black-and-white or generic ATS templates, AI-Letter utilizes carefully curated typography and modern styling presets (Georgia serif vs. system sans-serif) with unique backgrounds (Nova slate dark mode, Milano warm ivory, Nordic cool blue, and Onyx gold accents) that immediately stand out to recruiters while remaining 100% ATS-friendly.

### 2. Magic AI Resume Writer (Voice & Text)
* Candidates can dictate or type their background in a freeform conversational format. The backend AI parses the text, extracts key skills, achievements, dates, and institutions, and automatically populates the corresponding resume fields.

### 3. Strict Multimodal CV Parsing
* PDF CV uploads are analyzed by Google Gemini to extract full employment histories, projects, education, certifications, awards, publications, and interests, retaining the original document language.

### 4. Side-by-Side ATS Keyword Optimization
* Integrates a scoring engine that parses job descriptions and cross-references them with the candidate's CV to output missing keywords, ATS score breakdowns, and AI-optimized achievement bullet points.

### 5. Automated Follow-Ups & Interview Preparation
* Generates 8 custom interview questions and suggested answers formatted using the STAR methodology (Situation, Task, Action, Result) based on the specific job vacancy and cover letter.
* Automatically schedules and drafts polite follow-up messages based on the elapsed time since the job application.

---

## Module Breakdown
1. **Landing & Conversion Page**: Modern dark theme page with bento-grid features overview, live AI-tool mockups, transparent pricing tables, and deep-linked templates library.
2. **Onboarding**: A brief conversational flow to capture the candidate's profession, experience, and key skills to build their initial profile.
3. **Dashboard Command Center**: Aggregates total applications, waiting responses, upcoming interviews, and recent documents.
4. **CV Maker**: An interactive drag-and-drop/reorderable resume constructor with direct-to-browser PDF rendering.
5. **Cover Letter Tab**: AI-tailored cover letter drafts supporting different tones (Professional, Creative, Direct) and length presets.
6. **Job Tracker**: A simplified pipeline database to track application dates, company status changes, and follow-ups.
7. **Pro & Rewards Tab**: Premium subscription checkouts and referral rewards (sharing on LinkedIn/Twitter to earn free generations).
